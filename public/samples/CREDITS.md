# Sample credits

Every sample in this folder comes from one of two free libraries by Versilian Studios, both released under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** public domain dedication. They can be used, changed and shared for any purpose without asking or paying.

- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)**: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
- **VSCO 2 Community Edition** by Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner) and Ivy Audio (Simon Dalzell): https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (homepage: https://vis.versilstudios.com/vsco-community.html)

Thank you to Versilian Studios and everyone who recorded for these libraries. VSCO's readme asks that the samples not be sold on their own and that improvements stay open, and Steplab follows that.

## What was changed

One recording was chosen per instrument. Quiet recordings were first raised to full level. Each was trimmed to a few seconds (with its leading silence removed so hits land on the beat), faded out, mixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz, 16-bit, and peak-normalized. The root note of each melodic sample was measured with a pitch detector, because the libraries name notes one octave lower than scientific pitch.

`manifest.json` lists every file with its name, group, root note and source library.
